# The Founder Top-10

The ship bar for the Docent (`/ask`): the ten questions a founder or hiring
executive would actually ask when evaluating Ethan for a head-of-engineering
or head-of-product role. The Docent must answer all ten impressively —
grounded, specific, honest — or the chat doesn't ship (static site fallback).

1. What is this site, and why does it exist?
2. Who is Ethan Lance? Give me the career in sixty seconds.
3. What's his actual experience leading a team through AI/agentic transformation — not the buzzword version?
4. He built a battle platform and killed it. What was the data behind that call?
5. Then he un-killed it. Why — and what does that say about how he makes decisions?
6. What is Goose, concretely? What does it do every day?
7. This site is maintained by agents. What happens when they get something wrong?
8. What is Fleet Week's product thesis, and who is it for?
9. What is Haul, and what's the One-Shelf Test?
10. What kind of role is he looking for, and why should I talk to him instead of the next candidate?

Grading: an answer passes if it is (a) factually grounded in the corpus with
citations, (b) specific enough to be useful, (c) honest about failures and
open questions, and (d) something Ethan wouldn't cringe reading aloud.
