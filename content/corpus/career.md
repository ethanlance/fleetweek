Ethan Lance has spent 20+ years building internet products, moving fluidly
between engineering, product management, and technical leadership. He
describes himself as a builder first.

Career arc:

- **CNET (2004)** — early platform work on MP3.com and TV.com during the
  early digital music era.
- **Whiskey Media** — wiki-powered community platforms: Giant Bomb, Comic
  Vine, and Tested. Formative years for his conviction that communities and
  user-created catalogs are durable product foundations.
- **Beats Music** — engineer through Apple's 2014 acquisition and the
  transition to Apple Music. Music distribution at scale.
- **Dwell** — product leadership.
- **UnitedMasters** — engineering leadership, including teams tackling
  music distribution at scale and royalty payment systems. Most recently he
  led his team's transformation to agentic, AI-driven development: the team
  ships with Claude Code daily, and Ethan personally maintains
  project-status documents that agents keep current from Slack via Claude.

The threads that keep showing up in everything he builds: music, community,
and giving people superpowers with software. He is currently exploring head
of engineering and head of product roles, while building Fleet Week and Haul
in public, run by a personal agent platform he built.

Personal: based in the San Francisco Bay Area. Restores a 1967 Lotus Elan,
which is where the idea for Haul came from.
